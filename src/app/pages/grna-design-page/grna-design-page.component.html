Method & scope

SpCas9: 20-nt spacer and canonical NGG PAM, both strands. Cut is represented as a boundary 3 bp upstream of PAM. Optional regions restrict the cut position, not primer coverage. Shared mode requires an exact spacer in every selected reference; reference-specific mode excludes candidates with any other PAM-valid input site within the chosen mismatch count.

Other-site counts are exhaustive for the supplied sequences, canonical PAM and ungapped substitution limit, for each checked candidate. N-containing sites are skipped; bulges, noncanonical PAMs and sequences outside the input are not screened. Zero other sites does not establish genome-wide specificity. GC and region distance are transparent sorting rules, not predicted activity scores. Coding regions are not inferred from raw FASTA.

Design practice: IDT guide design · CRISPOR methods. This implementation does not run CRISPOR or its efficiency/off-target models. Wheat example: TaPDS identifiers, Chinese Spring IWGSC GCA_900519105.1.
