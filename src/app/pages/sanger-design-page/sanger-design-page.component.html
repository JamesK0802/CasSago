Method & references

Accepts full genomic or gene sequences (up to 2 Mb each; 8 Mb total), generates candidates around the required target windows, and checks binding across the full input. Shared-Sanger searches exact shared pairs first, then an exact shared sequencing primer with reference-specific opposite primers. If needed, it tries one mismatch per reference with positions within 3 bp, then one mismatch with a larger position spread, and finally exactly two mismatches per reference. Mismatch counts must be equal across all intended references. The terminal 10 bases at the 3′ end must always match exactly. For single mismatches, closer positions across references rank first, followed by earlier 5′ positions; no candidate exceeds two mismatches. PCR product length and sequencing read range are separate limits. Product length includes both primers; read distance begins after the sequencing primer. Every required window must fit within the usable read range, with different distances to corresponding targets. Only the sequencing side must have different target distances. An exact opposite primer is required for each reference; a common opposite primer is preferred. It is not an additional sequencing primer. Separate-reference mode designs individual PCR pairs with a configurable cross-binding mismatch screen.

Offset separation and WT sequence differences are design diagnostics. They do not prove that mixed edited reference traces can be decomposed. These design diagnostics are separate from trace fitting in the Sanger Analysis tab.

CHOPCHOP uses Primer3 and genomic mapping for primer design. This browser search screens the supplied references, not a whole genome. Shared designs check at most 600 primary primers, retain multiple partner options and return up to 300 pairs. Progressive shared candidates require at least five resolved mismatches at all unintended ungapped sites across the supplied references and both strands. Unknown bases prevent a confirmed pass. This is not a genome-wide binding guarantee. Relax conditions advances one search stage at a time. Select the sequencing primer first, then a complete opposite-primer set; a shared opposite primer is preferred. Mismatches are shown in red on the primer map and its hover sequence. Mismatch primers are incorporated into predicted PCR products; amplification balance requires experimental validation. PCR-only ranking is unchanged. Sanger coverage excludes at least the first 50 bp and the final 50 bp of the shorter of the configured read limit and predicted extension. The whole target window plus indel allowance must fit inside this usable region. Measured quality may require a larger exclusion.

Candidate generation and oligo checks follow principles used by Primer3 and Primer-BLAST; this browser implementation does not run either tool. SantaLucia 1998 supplies the Tm model. TIDE and Poly Peak Parser provide related trace-decomposition work, not validation of this proposed assay.

Separate-reference design also draws on the locus-specific design strategy described in PolyMarker; this prototype does not run PolyMarker.
