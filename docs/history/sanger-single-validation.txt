Single-first Sanger validation (2026-10-29)

Stage order: single reference / single target -> homeolog / single target ->
homeolog / multiple targets. Do not advance solely on a high reconstruction R2
or a low error among a small subset of accepted cases. The overall single-stage
gate remains open because complete allele-sequence reconstruction is not yet
reliably resolved for some multi-allelic single-locus mixtures.

Implemented single net-spectrum corrections:
* Filter weak or impure WT-template cycles using a 25-cycle called-base signal
  check, including traces without quality values. Never apply this purity test
  to the edited trace: genuine mixtures naturally have secondary peaks.
* Use positive sparse WT-contrast fitting, standardized by each column's
  contrast norm and upstream noise, with an unpenalized support refit.
* Replace the arbitrary 60/82-row floor with a 30-cycle floor and a conservative
  observations-to-parameter bound. Retain all requested indel classes; also
  require numerical identifiability and stability of the entire net spectrum.
* A few isolated discordant cycles remain in the objective and raw R2. Dense
  or sustained mismatch still withholds quantitative estimates. This is a
  net-size gate and does not certify individual allele sequences.
* Annotation estimability follows quantitative QC instead of always being true.
* Target window is a reporting setting, not the fluorescence decomposition span.
