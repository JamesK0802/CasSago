v42 conditional Sanger decoder checkpoint, 5 November 2026

Scope: fixed three-reference, two-forward-target synthetic panel. Each target/reference contains WT plus five distinct edited groups, with independently generated event sequences, unequal proportions and molecular pairings. Origins are within cut ±3 bp; insertion/deletion lengths are 1–10 bp. This checkpoint does not establish general recovery on noisy experimental traces.

Frozen prospective gate: 5 consecutive datasets passed. Across 150 non-WT groups, 121 matched directly by complete target sequence and within ±2 percentage points; 29 were accepted only with strict complete-signal or independently re-rendered native control+edit equivalence certificates. Unexplained 0; WT 30/30 accepted; false extra groups >=1pp 0. Candidate/option membership alone receives no credit. Ground truth is read after inference only.

Changes: half-open integer ADC rounding intervals prevent adjacent observed bins from sharing an excluded upper endpoint. A 1e-5 ADC numerical upper interior is used. Near-coincident allele frequencies from different net-length classes can be perturbed in bounded proposals; every proposed sequence dictionary is freely refitted to the full measured signal and must improve the same measured objective. Equal-length transfers retain the previous invariant-marginal behavior.
