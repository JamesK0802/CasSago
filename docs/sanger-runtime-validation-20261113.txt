Change

Official cvxjs bindings were dropping complete primal/dual witnesses unless the termination status was exactly Solved. Finite full-sized witnesses are now returned under other statuses, but acceptance still requires independent ORIGINAL primal feasibility and global dual gap <=1e-7. No genotype truth, separately inferred profile, extra noise, or allele cap enters inference.

Equivalent positive objective scaling multiplies both P and c. Dual multipliers are converted back BEFORE checking the original model. The units portfolio tries a tighter equivalent objective before exhausting precision-only retries. Failed certificates remain failures. Interrupted work remains pending.
