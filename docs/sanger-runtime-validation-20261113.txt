Conservative geometry envelopes and zero-objective proof (2026-11-14, release candidate)
The optional geometry LP strengthens a global outer bound; its optimizer point is never an inferred genotype. It previously attempted full LP optimality even when the objective was identically zero, and repeatedly retried difficult geometry objectives after failed exact certificates. It now derives a lower bound from finite signed row multipliers plus ALL input-variable box corrections, with compensated accumulation and an outward roundoff guard. A short common-WASM LP run proposes multipliers; incomplete/invalid proposals never become optimizer solutions. A zero objective returns its universal lower bound0 without solving a feasibility LP. Invalid runtime/witnesses retain the independently valid box proof. TwoSum plus an outward objective correction also covers duplicate-term cancellation, including a falsely rounded zero; no unsafe zero objective is certified. Cache keys preserve exact coalesced coefficients, are bounded to16 entries and invalidate changed domains; zero objectives do not occupy cache entries. Optional native-workspace disposal errors are contained.
The global certificate keeps the tighter of this projected bound and the pre-existing ORIGINAL equality/box certificate. The full nonlinear confidence model, observations, hypotheses, noise allowance and genotype retrieval remain unchanged. Primary fits, representative selection and ordinary exact optimizers STILL require original primal feasibility and original global dual gap<=1e-7. A conservative geometry lower envelope is explicitly NOT an attained optimizer solution. No tolerance is relaxed to accept an unfinished fit.
